Students / CRM

Open a student to review the complete master record, personal/employment data, academic history, exemptions, results and operational records.

DEMO.xlsx sample loaded: 28 student records. Click Open Full Master Record on any student to view the complete imported profile and academic row.
Backend result convention: a result of 1 means the module has been attempted or registered but is still outstanding. A blank module/date means it has never been attempted. Open a student record to view the full CLAW / CCA / CG / BDY / IFAI / DOS / RM / STAX paired backend layout.

Programme Management

Configure programmes independently from registration periods.

Set each programme up once. New June / November cycles are created as intakes and inherit the programme structure automatically. Archived programmes remain in historical records but are hidden from normal operations.
Role permissions will be added later. Intended design: Admin cannot permanently delete programmes; permanent deletion is reserved for Super Admin.

Session / Attendance Offerings

Separate registration periods for attendance-based programme requirements such as Singapore Taxation (STAX).

Only offerings with status Open are shown to eligible students. Scheduled, Draft and Closed offerings stay hidden from student registration.

Eligibility & Document Review

Review applications, supporting documents, OpenCerts checks and additional-document requests.

Transcript requirements are conditional. An applicant may state that their qualification does not issue an official transcript; this should be reviewed, not automatically treated as a missing document.

Document Requests

Existing Student Account Linking

Registered Applicants

Full registration records for new and existing students, including personal details, modules, sponsorship, payment, receipts, verification, confirmation and cancellation.

Exams & Results

Manage results by examination intake and module, with bulk mark upload and controlled release.

Two-step result control: Step 1 — upload and validate marks, then submit the batch for approval. Step 2 — a different checker approves and releases the batch. Students do not see uploaded results before release.

Individual Result / Attempt Records

Used for exceptional manual corrections, historic attempts, absences and approved deferments.

Deferments

Review student-submitted deferment requests, supporting documents, fee decisions and target exam diets.

Submission rule: a deferment request can only be submitted by the student through the portal and must include a supporting document. Admin may record a manual override, but a reason and target exam diet are mandatory and audit logged.

Target Diet Carry-forward

Approved deferments that must be visible when planning the next examination diet.

Payments & Receipts

Upload payment proof, reconcile the payment record, then approve and issue the receipt as a separate step.

Two-step control: Step 1 reconciles the payment. Step 2 approves the reconciliation and generates the receipt. A payment shortfall requires explicit admin approval before registration confirmation can be sent.

Materials & IRC

Control which LMS materials students can see and whether each resource can be read online, downloaded, or both.

Only Active resources are visible in the student portal. Set access to Read Online Only when a material must not be downloaded.
